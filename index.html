SHO'T LEFT

Jozi traffic. One old white taxi. One owner who wants his money.

Coming soon to Google Play

Sho't Left is a pixel-art taxi runner on South Africa's busiest roads. Swipe between lanes, jump the potholes, dodge the traffic and pick up passengers from both pavements. Make the owner's target before the shift ends, and every rand after that is yours.

What's in it

  • Jozi by day, PTA by nightStages across Joburg and Pretoria, each with its own skyline, traffic and radio chatter, with more on the way.
  • Boss fightsThe Rank Bully, the Blue Light Brigade, the Gautrain and more, each changing the way you drive.
  • RoadblocksBuy the officer a coke, or try your luck with a full taxi.
  • Squeeze in moreBolt in an extra bench and overload past 15 for extra cash, if you can keep everyone on board.
  • Endless modeRandom areas, high scores, your longest run, and rewards to unlock.
  • No ads, if you likeOne optional purchase, Buy the Dev a Brannas, removes every ad. Act 3 and Act 4 are coming in early 2027, free for Brannas owners.
  • Make it yoursTaxis, liveries, paint, rims, trails, upgrades, power-ups, and a few secrets for drivers who keep their eyes open.
