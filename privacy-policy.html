Sho't Left Privacy Policy

Effective 28 November 2026

Sho't Left is made by G.h0st Dev. The game itself doesn't collect personal information. It shows ads from Google AdMob and sells one optional purchase through Google Play, and those Google services collect some data as described below.

What the game stores

Your progress, takings, upgrades, cosmetics, high scores and settings are saved only on your own device, in the app's local storage. They are not sent to us.

You can erase this data at any time with Settings → Reset progress in the game, or by uninstalling the app.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads between some runs, and optional ads you can choose to watch for a second chance or a free roadblock skip. These are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • approximate location, worked out from your IP address
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostic information such as crash and performance data

Google uses this for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Android settings.

Purchases (Google Play)

"Buy the Dev a Brannas" is a one-off purchase that removes ads. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details; we only learn from Google Play whether the purchase was made, so the game can turn ads off and restore the purchase on a new phone.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts, sign-ins or contact details.
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts or files access.
  • We don't sell your information to anyone.

Permissions

Internet access is used only to load ads and check purchases. Vibration is used to buzz your phone on crashes and pick-ups, and can be turned off in the game's Settings.

Children

Sho't Left is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights (POPIA)

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act you can ask what personal information is held about you and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. The game itself holds nothing about you off your device; for data collected by Google, use your Android settings or Google's own tools, or contact us and we'll help.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes what it collects or shares, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
